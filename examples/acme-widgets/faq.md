# FAQ

## How many widgets can I create for free?

Fifty, in the sandbox. Upgrade to the Team plan for 5,000.

## What is a variant?

A specific size and color combination of a widget. Each widget can have several variants,
each with its own price and stock level.

## Why did my request return 429?

You exceeded your plan's quota. Slow down and retry, or upgrade for a higher limit.
